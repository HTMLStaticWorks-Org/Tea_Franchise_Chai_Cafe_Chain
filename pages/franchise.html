Partner Profile

Franchise Eligibility & Site Checklist

We look for passionate partners committed to quality, hygiene, and community hospitality.

Investor Readiness

Financial & Management Criteria

• Liquid investment capability of ₹8.5 Lakhs to ₹28 Lakhs depending on chosen format.
• Commitment to ethical staff practices and strict adherence to zero-premix brewing SOPs.
• Prior retail, hospitality, or business management experience is advantageous but not mandatory.

Property Specifications

Commercial Property Requirements

• Ground-floor commercial property (owned or leased) with minimum 12 ft. clear road frontage.
• 10 KW to 18 KW three-phase electrical load and dedicated potable water inlet & drainage.
• Located near high-footfall retail markets, tech parks, universities, transit hubs, or hospitals.
