Nutrition & Allergen Advisory

Full ingredient transparency across our dairy, nut, gluten, and sweetener selections.

Our classic milk chais are brewed with ethically sourced A2 cow milk or rich buffalo milk. For vegan and lactose-intolerant guests, all hot and iced chais can be prepared in separate dedicated kettles using unsweetened barista oat milk or almond milk, and our Sulaimani and Hibiscus Cold Brews are 100% dairy-free.

Royal Rajwadi Chai, Shahi Tukda, and Baked Gulab Jamun Cheesecake contain pistachios and almonds. Bakehouse snacks (Bun Maska, Vada Pav, Samosa, Khari Puff) contain wheat gluten. Please inform our brewmaster of any severe nut or gluten allergies before ordering.

A standard 180ml unsweetened A2 milk kulhad chai contains approximately 78 kcal. Choosing organic Kolhapuri cane jaggery or Tamil Nadu palm jaggery adds trace minerals (iron and magnesium) with a lower glycemic spike than refined white sugar.

Questions About Our Brews

Menu Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our kulhad hygiene, take-home flasks, and catering.

Every terracotta kulhad is 100% single-use. Before pouring, each cup is steam-sanitized and UV-treated. After use, cups are collected in our Terracotta Return Bins and crushed for agricultural soil aeration and garden composting.

For takeaway and office meetings, we pack freshly brewed chai in food-grade thermal aluminium-lined kraft flasks (serving 5 or 10 cups) accompanied by bubble-wrapped terracotta kulhads so your chai stays piping hot at 82°C+ for up to 75 minutes.
