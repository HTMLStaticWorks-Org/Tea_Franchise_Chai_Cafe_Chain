Provenance & Craft

The Farm-to-Kulhad Sourcing Journey

Swipe or scroll across our four ethical supply pillars—from Brahmaputra river valley tea estates to traditional potter wheels.

01 • Tinsukia, Assam

Second-Flush Estate Plucking

Two leaves and a bud are hand-plucked at dawn from alluvial Brahmaputra gardens for a rich, malty amber liquor.

02 • Idukki Hills, Kerala

Sun-Cured Whole Spices

8mm bold green cardamom pods, Wayanad black pepper, and true cinnamon bark are sun-dried and stone-pounded daily.

03 • Ri-Bhoi & Kolhapur

Heirloom Ginger & Cane Jaggery

High-oil Meghalaya Ing-Makhir ginger and chemical-free Kolhapuri golden sugarcane jaggery deliver natural warmth.

04 • Khurja & Bankura

Hand-Thrown Terracotta Kulhads

Shaped on potter wheels from river clay and kiln-fired without toxic glazes for authentic sondhi aroma.
