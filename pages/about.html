Est. Monsoon 2018

The Spark Behind Chai Kulhad Co.

During a 2017 train journey through Uttar Pradesh and Assam, founders Raghavendra Joshi and Vasundhara Iyer noticed a bittersweet shift: railway platforms and urban tea stalls had replaced fragrant terracotta kulhads with wax-lined paper cups, while instant premix powders were replacing freshly pounded ginger and cardamom.

In July 2018, they partnered with 18 master kumhar families in Khurja and opened a 220 sq. ft. brass-kettle chai bar on Indiranagar's 100 Feet Road in Bengaluru. Within 90 days, queues stretched down the block—proving that modern India craved authentic, unhurried, hygienically brewed kulhad chai.

The Chai Kulhad Co. Sustainability Pledge

Conventional "paper" tea cups are coated with a thin layer of polyethylene plastic that takes over 30 years to break down. At Chai Kulhad Co., every hot beverage is poured into an unglazed, natural river-clay kulhad.

410+ Families Potter Cluster Livelihoods
100% Recycled Crushed for Urban Soil

Used kulhads dropped into our store collection bins are sanitized, crushed into porous terracotta grit (cocopeat alternative), and donated to city nurseries and organic rooftop farms.
